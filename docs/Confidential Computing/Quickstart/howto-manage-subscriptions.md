Contact a sales representative to subscribe to TA, change service limits
and quotas, or cancel your subscription.

## Subscription levels

TA offers Starter and Enterprise subscription plans. Each plan has
different service limits and quotas.

| Plan                                    | Maximum attestation API keys (workloads) | Maximum management API keys | Maximum tenant admins | Maximum tenant users | Policies |
|:----------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------:|:---------------------------:|:---------------------:|:--------------------:|:--------:|
| Starter                                 |                    1                     |              2              |           2           |         100          |    10    |
| Enterprise — Base configuration         |                    5                     |              2              |           5           |         100          |    10    |
| Enterprise — one additional workload    |                    6                     |              2              |           5           |         100          |    15    |
| Enterprise — three additional workloads |                    8                     |              2              |           5           |         100          |    20    |
| Enterprise — five additional workloads  |                   10                     |              2              |           5           |         100          |    25    |
| Enterprise — custom                     |                   100                    |              2              |           5           |         100          |    50    |

### Change your subscription level

You can change or cancel your subscription at any time. Contact your
sales representative to make changes.

## Register and sign in with a third-party identity provider

When a new user is registered with TA, TA sends an invitation email
containing a link. Complete these steps to register:

1. Select the link in the invitation email.
1. Enter your email address and authenticate with your credentials. If
   your email address is not associated with a third-party identity
   provider, TA sends a code to your email address. Use the code to sign
   in.
1. Accept the guest consent agreement.
1. Register for multifactor authentication. Use a time-based
   authenticator app, such as Microsoft Authenticator\* or Google
   Authenticator\*, or receive a one-time passcode by text or voice call.
1. Read and accept the Terms of Use.
1. In the TA Portal, select **Continue** to acknowledge that you have
   read the Community Terms and Service Agreement.

### Sign in as a returning user

To sign in to the TA Portal:

1. Open the Client Portal.
1. Enter your email address and password.
1. Complete authentication with your authenticator app or a one-time
   passcode sent by text or voice call.

# Writing Samples from Various Jobs

## 1) Software Engineer
I improved API latency by profiling slow endpoints, introducing response caching, and reducing duplicate database calls. Average response time dropped from 420ms to 170ms over two releases.

## 2) Registered Nurse
During a high-volume shift, I coordinated with physicians and support staff to prioritize patient care using acuity-based triage. Our unit reduced average wait time for urgent cases by 18% that month.

## 3) Construction Project Manager
I managed a multi-phase renovation by sequencing subcontractor work, updating risk logs weekly, and tightening materials tracking. The project was delivered on schedule with a 3% cost underrun.

## 4) Teacher
I redesigned a unit plan around formative assessments and small-group instruction to better support mixed skill levels. End-of-term proficiency scores rose by 12 percentage points.

## 5) Customer Support Specialist
I analyzed recurring ticket themes and created a troubleshooting playbook for the top ten issues. First-contact resolution improved from 61% to 79% in one quarter.

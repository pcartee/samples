Platform Capacity Approval Process

This document provides an end-to-end guide for the Supply Buildout Process at Apple's Cloud Infrastructure Build Operations (CIBO). It covers how platform capacity requests are reviewed, approved, and fulfilled.

Purpose​

This process governs the review and approval of platform capacity requests initiated by Lines of Business (LOBs) and non-foundational platforms (KCS and ADP) submitted to platform teams (for example, Compute, Storage, Data Services) for fulfillment.

Process Flow​

note

Steps one through six are sequential and must be performed in the order presented.

Capacity request submission​

Owner: LOB Capacity Requestor

  • The LOB requester notifies their Demand Planner of a platform capacity request.
  • The LOB requester must include all required details: business driver, platform, resource type (for example, vCPU, RAM, sellable unit), quantity, need-by date, and business justification.

Output: Email submitted to the LOB Demand Planner.

LOB demand planner review​

Owner: LOB Demand Planner (See People Matrix below.)

  • Validates the completeness and accuracy of the request.
  • Checks with Platform Capacity DRI to determine if the necessary capacity is available for the request.
  • Translates the platform resource request into a capital expenditure (CapEx) estimate.
  • The LOB Demand Planner prepares an approval request email with all required details, including business driver, resource type, quantity, need-by date, justification, and CapEx estimate.
  • The email must include the following stakeholders: Capacity Requestor, LOB Capacity DRI, LOB Financial Controller, CIBO Platform Demand Planner, Platform Capacity DRI, Infrastructure Finance, and ASE Capacity EPMs.

Output: Email sent to the LOB Capacity DRI and financial controller requesting approval.

LOB capacity DRI review​

Owner: LOB Capacity DRI (See People Matrix below.)

  • Reviews the request for alignment with strategic business priorities and capacity objectives.
  • Verifies the timing and accuracy of resource requirements.
  • Approves, modifies, or requests clarification as needed.

Output: The LOB Capacity DRI provides feedback via email on an as-needed basis and sends an approval email.

LOB financial controller review​

Owner: LOB Financial Controller (See People Matrix below.)

  • Reviews financial implications of the request.
  • Validates budget alignment with fiscal constraints and forecasts.
  • Approves, rejects, or requests modification to ensure financial compliance.

Output: The LOB Financial Controller provides feedback via email on an as-needed basis and sends an approval email.

Notify platform stakeholders of approved demand​

Owner: LOB Demand Planning Team

  • Shares the email of the fully approved request with the Platform Demand Planner and Platform Capacity DRI.
  • Includes a breakdown by resource type (for example, vCPU, GPU, TB Storage), need by month, and data center(s).

Output: Email sent to the Platform Demand Planner and Platform Capacity DRI notifying them of approved demand.

Platform acknowledgment, review, and response​

Owner: Platform Capacity DRI

  • Reviews the approved request for feasibility against existing platform constraints (for example, supply availability, deployment timelines, architectural dependencies).
  • Notifies the LOB Demand Planner and other stakeholders if any issues or risks may impact timely fulfillment.

Output: Email acknowledging receipt of the approved demand and highlighting any constraints or risks related to fulfillment.
